Support

Stockyard — the fantasy stock league

Stockyard is a simulated ("paper") trading game: build virtual portfolios with fictional money and compete with friends in private leagues. No real money is ever invested.

Contact us

For help, questions, feedback, or account issues, email stockyard.app@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 1–2 days.

Reporting a player

If another player's name, photo, or activity is abusive or inappropriate, report it in the app: open their profile page and tap the … button, or long-press their row on the community board. You can also block a player there, which keeps their activity out of your feeds. We review reports within 24 hours. You can reach us at the email above as well.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, portfolios, trades, and league data.

Purchases

Stockyard is free. There are no in-app purchases and no subscription. If that changes in a future version, anything paid would be billed through your App Store account and managed in your device's subscription settings.
